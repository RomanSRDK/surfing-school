Who We Are

VILAMOURA SURF SCHOOL

  • Algarve
  • South Coast
  • Perfect Waves for Your Experience

The school was founded in 2006 as the first surf school at Vilamoura Beach in the Algarve. Our school is certified by the Portuguese Surfing Federation and Turismo de Portugal. We aim to teach the art of surfing in a safe, correct, and relaxed way, always under the close and attentive supervision of our instructors. Thousands of people have already learned how to ride waves on a surfboard with us.

Our facilities allow students to get changed, safely store their belongings, and enjoy a warm shower after class. We offer a wide range of softboards and modern wetsuits, providing a safe and enjoyable learning experience.
Our instructors are highly trained, experienced, and accredited by the Portuguese Surfing Federation. The School is open every day of the year, including weekends and holidays. Hours vary depending on the tides and sea conditions.

YOUR BEST ADVENTURE

Welcome to our school! If you've come this far, it's because you're interested in taking your first steps in surfing. It's never too late to start riding the waves — you'll love this new lifestyle! Surfing improves your physical and mental health in a relaxed and fun environment.
Vilamoura Surf School also offers group and private lessons in surfing, stand-up paddleboarding, longboarding, bodyboarding, skimboarding, and Carver skateboarding. If you're looking to rent equipment, we have high-performance boards and wetsuits.
